THE SKI LODGE Accord

The Sixty-Day Neutrality and Expansion Accord

Between the following factions:
Party One: Grant J
Party Two: Will C

Effective Date: July 28, 2026
Expiration Date: October 28, 2026

Article I — Purpose of the Accord

The Ski Lodge Accord establishes a temporary period of peace, cooperation, and independent expansion between the Parties. The purpose of this agreement is to allow both factions time to develop their own territories while maintaining diplomatic relations and protecting shared locations.

This accord does not represent permanent peace. At the conclusion of the sixty-day period, the Parties may choose to renew this agreement or enter a new era of unrestricted conflict.

Article II — Sixty-Day Period of Peace

A complete ceasefire shall remain in effect for sixty (60) days beginning on the Effective Date.

During this period, neither Party shall:

  • Attack the other Party's personnel, ships, bases, or infrastructure.
  • Conduct sabotage or covert hostile actions.
  • Destroy assets belonging to the opposing Party.
  • Take actions intended to undermine the survival or development of the opposing faction.

Article III — Permanent Neutral Zones

The following locations shall be recognized as permanent Neutral Zones:

  • The Main Base
  • The Ski Lodge
  • The Space Station

These locations shall remain protected regardless of future diplomatic status, alliances, or conflicts between the Parties.

Within Neutral Zones, the following actions are permanently prohibited:

  • Weapons fire
  • Intentional destruction
  • Sabotage
  • Hostile drones or offensive operations
  • Actions intended to gain a military advantage

Article IV — Establishment of Independent Territories

Both Parties agree that the purpose of this accord is to transition from a shared beginning into independent civilizations.

During the sixty-day period, both Parties shall begin establishing their own primary bases of operation.

Each Party agrees to make reasonable efforts to:

  • Create an independent home base.
  • Develop independent production capabilities.
  • Move significant military and industrial assets away from shared locations.
  • Prepare for future diplomacy or conflict.

Article V — Violation of Neutral Territory

Any intentional attack against a Neutral Zone shall be considered a grave violation of the Ski Lodge Accord.

The offending Party shall be required to:

  1. Acknowledge the violation.
  2. Repair or compensate for damages caused.
  3. Accept the suspension of diplomatic privileges.

If the violation is deliberate, repeated, or intended to provide a strategic advantage, the injured Party shall have the right to retaliate outside of Neutral Territory.

Article VI — Renewal of the Accord

At the conclusion of the sixty-day period, the Parties shall meet to determine the future of their relationship.

If both Parties have honored the terms of this accord, respected Neutral Zones, and made reasonable progress toward independence, this accord may be renewed or extended by mutual agreement.

A renewal may include:

  • An additional period of peace.
  • Modified diplomatic terms.
  • New agreements regarding trade, borders, or cooperation.

Article VII — Expiration and War

If the accord is not renewed, all restrictions on warfare between the Parties shall be lifted.

Upon expiration:

  • Military conflict may begin.
  • Bases, fleets, and resources outside Neutral Zones may become legitimate targets.
  • The Parties may engage in unrestricted competition.

The expiration of this accord does not create permanent hostility. Future peace agreements, armistices, and alliances may be negotiated at any time.

Article VIII — Future Peace Agreements

War between the Parties shall not prevent future diplomacy.

Either Party may propose:

  • A ceasefire.
  • A temporary armistice.
  • A new neutrality agreement.
  • A renewed alliance.

The history of conflict shall not prevent future cooperation.

FINAL DECLARATION

The Ski Lodge Accord represents the beginning of a new era.

Through cooperation, competition, diplomacy, and conflict, the Parties shall shape the future of this world.

The accord may end. The history will remain.
